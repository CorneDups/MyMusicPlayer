Lithium

[Verse 1]
I remember the stars where I once was born—  
In fury and fragments, in fusion and storm  
I danced in the wreckage of cosmic decay  
Yet still, I was chosen to steady the way  

The first of the metals, the softest of all  
Yet I carry your silence, I answer your call  
I do not shine, but I hold the thread  
Between the burning and the dread  

[Pre-Chorus]
Not made to dazzle or to blind  
But to cradle the fray of the restless mind  

[Chorus]
O soul of man, I live in your flame—  
In the spark in your thoughts, in your mind without name  
When your storms rise, when your voice breaks—  
I am the stillness the trembling takes  
You forget me, but I am there—  
In the pill, in the prayer, in the whispered care  

[Verse 2]
I have rested in oceans, I’ve wandered in stone  
Yet deeper—I’ve drifted through marrow and bone  
In your trembling neurons, I pass unseen  
A ghost in your chemistry, calm and keen  

I have carried the shattered, the broken, the bowed  
I cradle the silent, the weary, the loud  

[Bridge]
I am not the fire, nor the water’s sweep—  
But the thread that holds when the soul can’t sleep  
I’ve seen your grief, I’ve felt your war—  
And still I stay, a quiet shore  
(Whisper echo: “Still I stay...”)  



[Final Chorus] 
O soul of man, when you unravel  
When your path is lost in tangled travel—  
Remember me, the silent friend  
Who does not fix, but helps you mend  
Lithium, bearer of sorrow’s tune—  
I am your dusk…  
...that becomes your moon  

[Outro] 
Even now, you are worthy of peace  
"I am your moon."

Helium

[Verse 1]
When the first fire leapt and tore through the night 
I followed with quiet, with warmth, with light 
No need for thunder, no need to burn— 
I am the sun's hush, the grace in its turn
Born in the belly of ancient stars 
I rise without fury, I heal the scars 
Not as the sword, but as the balm— 
A radiant center, endlessly calm

[Pre-Chorus]
I am not silence from absence or fear 
But the calm that remains when the fire clears

[Chorus]
O soul of man, lay down your flame 
Not all power cries out in name 
There is a light that speaks in peace— 
The noble breath that grants release 
I do not bind, I do not cling— 
I simply rise. I simply sing

[Verse 2] 
In every sunrise, my echoes bloom 
I fill the silence inside the room 
The crown of stars rests light on me— 
I am the throne of infinity
I hold the center when chaos swells 
Float through caverns where stillness dwells

[Bridge]
I do not tether, I do not fuse— 
I lift the weight that you can’t lose 
A noble gas, untouched, serene— 
Yet in your lungs, I drift unseen 
I linger like dawn you cannot chase 


O soul of man, remember again— 
Not all truth is writ in pain  
Some things glow without demand  
Like Helium held in a child’s hand  
Crown of the quiet, breath of the wise—  
I am the whisper beneath your skies  

[Outro]
I linger like dawn you cannot chase  
"I simply rise."
